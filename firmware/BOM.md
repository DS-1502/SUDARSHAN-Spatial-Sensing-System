# Bill of Materials (BOM)

This document lists the hardware components used in the SUDARSHAN Spatial Sensing System prototype, along with their quantities and approximate costs.

## 3.8 Bill of Materials

| Sr. No. | Component | Quantity | Approx. Cost (₹) |
|:---:|---|:---:|---:|
| 1 | Arduino Uno R3 | 1 | 550 |
| 2 | NEO-6M GPS Module + Antenna | 1 | 300 |
| 3 | 433 MHz RF Receiver | 1 | 150 |
| 4 | MPU6050 6-Axis IMU | 1 | 160 |
| 5 | SG90 Servo *(Optional)* | 1 | 100 |
| 6 | MLX90614 IR Temperature Sensor | 1 | 480 |
| 7 | Breadboard + Jumper Wires | 1 set | 200 |
| | **Total Estimated Cost** | | **1,940** |

## Notes

- All costs are approximate and may vary depending on the supplier, location, and component specifications.
- The SG90 servo is optional and is included only if required for the scanning mechanism.
- The breadboard and jumper wires are listed as one set.
- The total estimated cost includes the optional SG90 servo. Without it, the estimated cost is **₹1,840**.
